Admission to Yoga Diploma Course, 2019-20 has been closed.

Official Circular

No. WBYA/Dip-Course/Yoga/2019/

DIPLOMA COURSE on YOGA in its therapeutic, theoretical and practical application aspects is going to be held shortly under the auspices of West Bengal Yoga Association. Willing candidates of both sexes, irrespective of their affiliation and in conformity with the rules and regulations and terms and conditions mentioned herein under, may apply for admission filling the prescribed form. The application forms will be available at the Association Office on any Saturday between 5 p.m. to 7 p.m. or on the website of the association.

Conditions to be observed:

  • Application for admission may be submitted through an affiliated unit or otherwise.
  • Qualification: Minimum Higher Secondary Examination passed or its equivalent.
  • The Course Fees: Rs. 2,500/- (Two thousand and five hundred) only with Enrollment Fees Rs. 200/- (Two hundred) only.
  • The application for admission is to be submitted in the office of the Association on any Saturday between 3 p.m. to 7 p.m. along with the course fees, age & address proof Certificate, Higher Secondary passed Certificate (Xerox) and 4 (Four) Stamp size photos within 20-07-2019.
  • Classes: Will be conducted on Saturday from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. & Sunday from 2 p.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Examination: Practical and Theoretical Examination will be held separately. Pass marks 40% both in theory and practical.
  • DIPLOMA will be awarded to the successful candidates after completion of the Course.
  • The DATE, VENUE and TIMING of the course will be notified.
  • Application form: May be collected from Association Office on paying Rs. 200/- (Two hundred) only as Enrollment Fees (including Prospectus).
  • After admission, Syllabus & Prospectus will be provided.
