About the Association

Preserving Yoga heritage and promoting Yoga sport across Bengal.

The West Bengal Yoga Association was created to preserve, expand and organize Yoga as a cultural practice, art and indigenous sport.

YOGA owes its origin to India, the land of great Rishis and Munis. Due to its manifold benefits for overall growth of an individual, in terms of body, mind and soul, it has been gaining worldwide recognition. Therefore, for the preservation and expansion of Yoga, the great ancient culture, art and indigenous sports of glorious India, an organization was required to be constituted at State level and National level. In view to the realization of that aim, the West Bengal Yoga Association came into existence.

Constituted: The West Bengal Yoga Association was constituted in the year 1972.

Registration: West Bengal Yoga Association is registered under the Societies Registration Act-XXVII of 1961 no. S/19280 of 1076-77.

Aims and Objectives

  • To promote, encourage, popularize, standardize and supervise Yoga in the Country.
  • To encourage the formation of District Yoga Associations in the Bengal.
  • To arrange and supervise the Yoga Championships, Camps, Seminars and other Contests.
  • To arrange, regulate and if necessary finance visits of the West Bengal Yoga Team in National Yoga Championship, Eastern India Yoga Championship, State Games etc.
  • To arrange, regulate and if necessary finance visits of the West Bengal Yoga team.
  • To perform all such others acts as may seem to the Association to be relevant and conducive to the attainment of the aims/objectives of the WBYA.
