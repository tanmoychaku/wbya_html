About the Association

A legacy of Yoga sport, culture and state representation.

West Bengal Yoga Association has guided state-level Yoga development, championship participation, training, district coordination and official representation for decades.

Role

Preparing competitors and connecting Bengal Yoga with the national stage.

State Representation

West Bengal Yoga Team first participated in the National Yoga Championship in 1974 at Patna, Bihar. Since then, the team has become 23 times winners and 18 times runners-up in National Yoga Championship.

The Association is responsible for preparing and supporting competitors for National Yoga Championship, Eastern India Chapter Yoga Championship, State Games Yoga Championship, State Yoga Championship and District Yoga Championship.

Selection and Training

Competitor selection is generally carried out on the basis of State Championship performance. When needed, special selection trials are held for National Yoga Championship, Eastern Yoga Championship, State Games and other competitions.

After selection, qualified competitors attend training camps supported by selected and experienced trainers.

Foundation

The first seeds were sown by Bharatshree Kamal Bhandari.

Bharatshree Kamal Bhandari first sowed the seeds for establishing a Yoga Sports Body at state level in 1972 at South Calcutta Physical Culture Association, Rash Behari Avenue, for promoting state Yoga sport in united Bengal.

With the support of Sri Gopal Chakraborty, Sri Hiten Roy, Sri Santosh Sanyal, Sri Dilip Banerjee, Sri Nirapada Pakhira and other honorable persons, the West Bengal Yoga Association came into existence.

Responsibilities

Work Undertaken by WBYA

  • Deciding the organization of State Championship.
  • Maintaining liaison between Yoga Federation of India, district associations and members.
  • Protecting the amateur status of competitors.
  • Arranging Diploma on Yoga course activities.
  • Promoting and developing Yoga across Bengal.
Affiliation

An apex Yoga Sports organization for Bengal.

WBYA is affiliated with Bengal Olympic Association and Yoga Federation of India. District Yoga Associations across Bengal assist with development, championships and preparation of competitors.
